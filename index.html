About Us

We are a community of Warhammer 3 Immortal Empires head-to-head PvP campaign players. Our goal is to bring together passionate players who enjoy the strategic depth and competitive nature of Total War campaigns. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a newcomer, you'll find a place here to hone your skills, share strategies, and participate in epic battles.

But the battle is far from straightforward—other factions with their own agendas and ambitions have entered the fray, turning the island into a battlefield of virtue and vice.
